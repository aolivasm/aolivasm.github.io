Antonio Olivas-Martinez

Hello, I’m Antonio.

I'm a physician and biostatistician, currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Center for Causal Inference in the Department of Biostatistics, Epidemiology and Informatics at the University of Pennsylvania. My background in mathematics, medicine, and biostatistics shapes my approach to methodological research, combining statistical rigor with an understanding of clinical and public health questions.

My research focuses on developing statistical methods for causal inference, particularly in settings involving unmeasured confounding. I am especially interested in sensitivity analysis with instrumental variables and proximal causal inference using proxy variables. My methodological work draws on semiparametric theory, flexible estimation methods, and the theory of inverse problems, with particular interests in estimation and regularization for ill-posed problems.

I also work on the development and validation of clinical prediction models, with a particular focus on predicting outcomes of in vitro fertilization (IVF) using large-scale registry data.
